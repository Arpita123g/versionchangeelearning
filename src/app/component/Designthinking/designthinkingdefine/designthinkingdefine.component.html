GAP Analysis

Battery Life

85% preference, but most brands offer only 1-2 days of battery life.

Gap: Extended battery life of 3-4 days.

Affordability

65% users prioritize this, but top-tier smartwatches are expensive.

Gap: A mid-range smartwatch with premium features.

Localization

60% showed preference, but few international brands cater specifically to Indian preferences.

Gap: Features like regional language support and local payment integration.

Constraints

Budget

Limited R&D funds compared to global competitors..

Manufacturing

Achieving a balance between quality and affordability in production.

Time

Rapidly evolving market; delay in launch might result in outdated technology.

Market Perception

Being perceived as a local brand might be a challenge when competing against international giants.

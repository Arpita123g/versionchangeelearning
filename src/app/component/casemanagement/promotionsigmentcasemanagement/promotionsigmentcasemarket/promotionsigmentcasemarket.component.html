In the current landscape of the Indian beauty market, estimated at a robust USD 27.23 billion, Herbal Inc. emerges as a trailblazer. Founded in 2008 amidst a market craving organic products, the visionary founders strategically navigated a hypercompetitive space dominated by cash-rich traditional players. Recognizing the power of branding, they pivoted in 2015, outsourcing manufacturing to concentrate on launching a diverse range of products, accumulating an impressive portfolio of 300 SKUs.

As the newly appointed Promotion Strategist, you inherit the responsibility for two recently launched products under the esteemed Herbal Inc. brand i.e., Acne Face Cream (30g) and Apple Cider Face Wash (100 ml). The communication mix budget allocated by the company is INR 10,000k while for other activities the quotes are provided by vendors. With a market research report highlighting competitors and consumer behaviour favouring organic growth, your task is to craft a promotion strategy for both offline and online channels, emphasizing Return on Advertising Spend (ROAS).

Despite a thinning profit margin and heightened competition, Herbal Inc.'s strengths lie in its established branding, rapid product launches, and influential market presence. Your role extends beyond promotions, delving into setting margins for three distinct channel partner categories. As consumer preferences align with Mordor Intelligence's report on the ascendancy of organic products, the branding team proposes a strategic shift, incorporating an environmentally friendly ethos into every promotional material. "To make products that are especially environmentally friendly, we curate our toxic-free, harmful chemical-free, paraben-free products with utmost care and precision. We are your one-stop shop for beauty, skin, hair, and baby care needs."

Mr. Raj recently started a paper manufacturing company, handling both finances and operations himself. Despite his diligent daily ledger recording, the bank statements show a different picture, indicating potential financial issues.

Upon investigation, a advisor identified several challenges affecting profitability. Firstly, incomplete ledger entries led to an inaccurate financial picture. Secondly, unclear cash flow management resulted in periods of negative cash flow, impacting financial stability. Lastly, personal and business expenses were not separated, further complicating financial assessment.

To address these challenges, the advisor suggested hiring a manager to provide clarity on financial utilization across operations, marketing, and business development. As a Senior Manager appointed to assist Mr. Raj, your role involves preparing financial statements (Balance Sheet, Income Statement, and Cash Flow Statement) to facilitate informed decision-making for the company's financial health and growth.

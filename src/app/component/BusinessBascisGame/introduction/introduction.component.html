How does it work?

Location

The decision regarding setting up your shop in one of the two locations from the given option must be decided based on various information.

Demand

On this page, a decision on forecasting demand must be taken. It will be a reference towards ordering the material and planning revenue.

Investment

Various options to make the experience better for customers are provided on this page. The decision on target market, product mix and quality are present.

Marketing

The price, promotion and service are the decision to be made on this page. These factors will impact the number of customers visiting your shop.

Reports

The performance of your decisions will be available in this section. It will provide a detailed report on the company’s performance as well as on your decision-making ability.

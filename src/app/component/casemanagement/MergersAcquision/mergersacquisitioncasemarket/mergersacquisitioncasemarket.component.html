Mumbai Auto Innovations (MAI)

Headquartered in the bustling metropolis of Mumbai, Mumbai Auto Innovations, popularly known as MAI, is a trailblazing company in the automobile sector. Founded in 1990 by Rajeev Mehta, an automotive enthusiast with a vision, the company has grown exponentially over the past three decades.

Vehicle Categories:

Combustion - Pride Series: MAI's combustion vehicles are known for their fuel efficiency and sleek designs. The Pride series, in particular, has been one of the top-selling models in several Indian states.

Hybrid-Fusion Series: Recognizing the need for eco-friendly solutions with combustion power, MAI introduced the Fusion series in 2005. These hybrids quickly became the choice for environmentally conscious consumers who weren’t yet ready to transition to full electric.

Electric - E-Spark Series: Launched in 2015, the E-Spark series represented MAI's foray into the rapidly emerging electric vehicle market. Boasting rapid charging and impressive range, the E-Spark models became instant favourites in urban areas.

Growth Challenges & Opportunities:

The automobile market in India and globally is becoming highly competitive. To stay ahead, MAI has identified three primary challenges:

Supply Chain Efficiency: With the advent of EVs, securing steady and sustainable supply chains for components, especially batteries, has been a challenge.

Increasing Market Share: While MAI is a recognized brand in India, it aims to increase its market presence in the Southeast Asian and Middle Eastern markets.

Cost Management: Like many automakers, MAI constantly seeks avenues to reduce manufacturing costs without compromising on quality.

A start-up named Dextrous Art was established last year in India. It was the brainchild of a group of artists who wanted to bring their creativity to people's homes. Dextrous Art specialized in selling artistic and home decor items that are unique, elegant, and affordable. The company has a great vision and a plan to capture a significant portion of the market. The home decor industry in India is booming, and the market size is estimated to be worth 121.97 billion Indian rupees by 2030. Dextrous Art wanted to tap into this growing market and make a name for itself.

However, the company is facing a major challenge in its planter sales. Despite using a pull strategy to attract customers, the sales are not meeting expectations. The stockouts are too high, and the company needs to solve the issue by tackling the ordering and inventory system. The company management is deliberating to use a push strategy to promote the planter products. The company could use social media platforms to create a buzz around its products and offer special deals to attract customers. This would create demand and encourage customers to purchase the products.

After analysing the company's sales data and inventory, the research team identified the problem. To solve this problem, the research team devised a forecasting system (patented). This system would simulate the inventory levels in real-time, making it easier to decide stock levels and reorder levels.

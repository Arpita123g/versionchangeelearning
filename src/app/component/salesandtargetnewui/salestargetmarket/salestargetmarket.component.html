Pablo, Inc., a medium-sized player in the soft drinks market, is on a mission to enhance its sales effectiveness in the vibrant Maharashtra region of India. In a dynamic market segment encompassing a variety of water-based beverages with flavouring additives, excluding coffee and tea-based drinks, the company is poised for growth. In 2023, the revenue in this segment stands at a substantial USD 8.70 billion, with a promising annual growth rate of 3% projected from 2023 to 2027. The intriguing facet of this market is its division into three key categories: Carbonated drinks, Energy and sports drinks, and Non-Carbonated drinks. Remarkably, Non-Carbonated drinks take the lead, contributing a significant 70% of the market's revenue.

The company has been successfully offering a range of four distinct Non-Carbonated soft drink products in the Maharashtra market for the past five years. Each of these products has been strategically positioned to cater to the unique preferences of consumer segments that have been carefully identified. The four consumer categories include "Easy Living" consumers, who value tradition, "Experiencers" who are open to trying new products regardless of reviews, "Hedonistic" individuals who seek pleasure from the flavour and richness of the product, and "Thinkers" who make well-informed decisions based on feedback and reviews from their trusted network.

To further complicate the landscape, these products are distributed through three distinct channels: Modern trade, Retail stores, and HORECA (hotels, restaurants, catering, cafés, bars). The challenge for the Sales Head role at Pablo, Inc. is not just to navigate the intricacies of a diverse market but also to align sales strategies with these customer segments and channels. Leveraging historical data and insights, the goal is to guide the sales team in achieving increased sales while optimizing costs. Success in this endeavour will require a deep understanding of consumer behaviours, market dynamics, and channel-specific nuances. Pablo, Inc.'s aim is to strengthen its market position and capture the hearts and taste buds of Maharashtra's diverse consumer base. This is where your expertise as a Sales Head will play a pivotal role, in shaping the company's trajectory in this exciting market."

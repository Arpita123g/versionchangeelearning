How does it work?

Market

The gaming world is evolving, and you must identify emerging needs. Dive into market reports to discern the crucial gaps in the market. Amidst varying trends and demands, decide on a specific genre or demographic to focus on.

Information Search

Armed with information about the market, you must embark on a fact-finding mission. But beware! Information can sometimes be misleading or contradictory, requiring you to judiciously discern key gamer desires.

Target

With insights in hand, decide the demographics, and who would be the main user of your game. The demographics have be to as detailed as possible taking into account the language, gender, and income.

Conceptualizing

It's time to brainstorm game concepts. Weigh the pros and cons of different genres, situational elements, cultural elements, and lifestyle elements. The choices made here will set the stage for the game's potential success or failure.

Crafting

Dive deep into the game launch process. Decide which channels you want to use to promote the game. You must choose a partner to publish the game or do it by your own. Create your revenue model.

Reports

The game hits the market, and it's time for a post-mortem. Analyze based on your decision-making how much market share you are able to achieve and how many paying customers.

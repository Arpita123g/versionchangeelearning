  • Outlook

  • In the bustling gaming landscape of India, mobile gaming reigns supreme with a staggering 360 million enthusiasts. A remarkable three out of every four gamers gravitate towards mobile games, placing India among the top five global contenders in the mobile gaming arena. Players, on average, dedicate over an hour daily to their gaming pursuits, using mobile phones as their primary device.

    Interestingly, an omni-channel approach is favoured, combining both online and offline experiences. As evening approaches, homes transform into gaming sanctuaries, offering players an uninterrupted experience during prime time. A dive into gaming habits reveals a versatile Indian gamer who is multi-genre-oriented, often playing more than twice a day.

    With an insatiable appetite for novelty, there's a strong inclination towards experimentation and exploring new games. The "free-to-play" model is the go-to choice for the majority.

    Shattering stereotypes, 45% of Indian gamers are female, with 58% of mothers identifying as gamers. Mobile gaming also appeals to the mature audience, with nearly a third aged 35 and above. When pitted against other forms of entertainment, gaming stands tall, with over 40% of gamers investing more than an hour daily, compared to the average 45 minutes spent on OTT platforms.

  • Communication-Channels

  • Social media, Shopping and Entertainment (Audio/ Video streaming) are the most popular activities undertaken by Males. Online banking and payments, News, Sports, and Food orders are not so popular for teens, but this category has much higher relevance for older age groups. Metro consists of 56% gamers with more time spent than non-metro.

    Social media, online shopping and listening to music are the popular activities done online by female gamers across age cohorts. Women in the age group of 20-34 years access online banking/wallets and watch Movies / TV shows as well on a large scale. Metro consists of 52% gamers with more time spent than non-metro.

  • Monetization

  • The mobile gaming sector predominantly garners its revenue through advertisements, with the current average earnings standing at Rs. 400 per thousand impressions. A variety of ad formats have found favour in this landscape, encompassing static and banner ads, rewarded video ads, interstitial video ads, and more engaging interactive ads. It's noteworthy that a significant 69% of gamers commit to watching ads in their entirety when lured by rewards. Supplementing this main revenue stream are in-app purchases, collaborations that offer exclusive rewards, and ad networks, the latter acting as mediators to optimize ad visibility. When exploring the game type hierarchy, we find free games at the base, followed by the more monetizable freemium games and pay-to-play games. Gamers inclined towards the latter two categories exhibit a spending range of Rs. 50 to 150 for their gaming experiences, with a clear trend indicating that older gamers have a heightened willingness to spend. A deeper dive into player preferences reveals an inclination towards personalized ads tailored to the game they're engaged with. Contextual ads that offer relevant content and interactive opportunities tend to resonate more, enhancing the overall user experience and, by extension, the monetization potential.
